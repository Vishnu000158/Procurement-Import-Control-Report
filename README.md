# Procurement-Import-Control-Report

### Chemical Procurement Spend & Sourcing Control Tower (Power BI)

*Author: Vishnu Pathak  
*Role: Purchase Executive & Procurement Data Analyst (Mumbai, India)  
*Tools Used:* Power BI Desktop (DAX, Power Query), Advanced Excel, Data Modeling

---

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Business Impact
An interactive 2-page *Power BI Executive Control Tower* designed to track chemical raw-material procurement (DMAP, HATU, Benzoic Acid) across domestic Indian suppliers (Mumbai, Vapi) and Chinese import hubs (Shanghai, Hangzhou, Nanjing):

* *Total Spend Analyzed:* ₹3.14+ Crore across domestic Ex-Works and international FOB/CIF orders.
* *Negotiated Cost Savings:* ₹14.57+ Lakhs (4.43% net savings below historical CRM benchmark prices).
* *Supplier Compliance:* Monitored 98.4% Vendor Fill Rate and 80.0% On-Time Delivery (OTIF) SLA across lead times.
